OUR STORY

Founded on a Simple Belief: Tradition Deserves a Global Stage

Chaarulaya began in 2011 in a small teaching room in Nagercoil, where a guru couple taught fifteen students Carnatic vocal and Bharatanatyam on weekend afternoons. Word travelled — first across the street, then across the ocean.

Today, Chaarulaya is a full academy spanning eight disciplines, fourteen empanelled gurus and students across twenty-four countries — but the room still feels small. Every student is still known by name.
