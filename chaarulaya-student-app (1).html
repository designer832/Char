About Us

About Us

Our story

Tradition Deserves a Global Stage

Chaarulaya began in 2011 in a small teaching room in Nagercoil, where a guru couple taught fifteen students Carnatic vocal and Bharatanatyam on weekend afternoons. Word travelled — first across the street, then across the ocean.

Today, Chaarulaya spans eight disciplines, fourteen empanelled gurus and students across twenty-four countries — but the room still feels small. Every student is still known by name.

Academy announcement

E.R. Ram Kumar joins the Senate of Manonmaniam Sundaranar University

With the blessings of his Guru, violinist and music composer E.R. Ram Kumar has been selected by the Hon’ble Governor & Vice Chancellor as a member of the Senate, for a period of three years.

AuthenticityTaught in unbroken lineage, never diluted for convenience.

JoyRigor and delight are not opposites; great teachers hold both.
